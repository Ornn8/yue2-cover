自动检查 / Automatic check

每个翻唱先用 Whisper（large-v3）逐字听写，把听到的原始文字和应唱的歌词都化成可比较的单位（日语化成假名读音、中文用汉字、英语用字母），再从头到尾对齐；只有连续两个以上相同的单位才算「听到」，单个字的巧合不算。一行达到「正确」要同时满足：听到的比例中文不低于 80%、日语和英语不低于 90%，并且起点落在位置上（按翻唱自身的速度拟合后误差 1 秒内）。日语和英语另请一个较弱的听写（Whisper small）复核，它也要听到该行至少 60%。只统计 Whisper 在原唱节选里同样听得清的歌词行，听不清原唱的行无法核验，不计入。喂给模型的歌词若含乱码（U+FFFD、拉丁字母乱码、半角片假名串、歌曲文字以外的字符）会在下单前被拒绝。展示条件：这些行里至少 60% 正确、覆盖率不低于 80%，并且主唱人声是单一声部（把主唱从成品里分离出来，左右声道不能明显不同步，否则像双声部叠唱）。每格最多试 3 次（个别格多试了几次），保留检查最好的一次；不满足条件的格标为「暂缺」、不放音频。Whisper 听唱歌的准确率有限，它的语言模型也会把含糊的发音「脑补」成正确的词，所以这仍只是粗筛，不能代替耳朵。

Each cover was transcribed with Whisper (large-v3); the raw text and the expected lyrics are both turned into comparable units (Japanese into kana readings, Chinese into characters, English into letters) and aligned end to end. A unit counts as heard only inside a run of two or more identical units, so isolated one-character coincidences do not count. A line is right when the share heard is at least 80% (Chinese) or 90% (Japanese, English) and it starts in place (within 1 s after fitting the cover's own tempo); for Japanese and English a second, weaker listener (Whisper small) must hear at least 60% of it too. Only lines Whisper also hears on the original excerpt are counted; lines it cannot verify even there are left out. Lyrics containing mojibake (U+FFFD, Latin-1 runs, half-width katakana runs, characters outside the song's scripts) are refused before an order is placed. A cell is shown when at least 60% of those lines are right, coverage is at least 80%, and the lead vocal is a single voice (the lead vocal is separated from the mix and must not be stereo-doubled, which sounds like a chorus). Each cell got up to 3 takes (a few got more) and the best by this check is kept; a cell that does not qualify is marked 暂缺 (withheld) with no audio. Whisper is imperfect on sung audio and its language model can fill a mumbled word in with the expected one, so this is a coarse filter, not a substitute for listening.
