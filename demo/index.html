自动检查 / Automatic check

每个翻唱用 Whisper 逐字听写，再与应唱的歌词逐行对齐：下表是「歌词行被听到、且起点落在位置上（按翻唱自身的速度拟合后误差 1 秒内）」的行数，以及字符覆盖率。Whisper 听唱歌的准确率有限，较短的歌词行容易被少听一两个字。Each cover was transcribed word by word with Whisper and aligned to the expected lines: lines heard and starting in place (within 1 s after fitting the cover's own tempo), and character coverage. Whisper is imperfect on sung audio, so short lines can lose a character or two. 每格最多试 3 次（个别格多试了几次），保留检查最好的一次；至少一半的歌词行在位、字符覆盖不低于 60% 才展示，否则该格标为「暂缺」、不放音频。Each cell got up to 3 takes (a few got more) and the best by this check is kept; a cell is shown only when at least half of its lines are in place and coverage is at least 60%, otherwise it is marked 暂缺 (withheld) with no audio.
